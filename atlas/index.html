Authority boundaries

Three distinctions behind governed effects.

AI systems can execute actions, observe outcomes, verify evidence and record facts. The difficult part is keeping those different acts from silently becoming the same authority.

This short series explains three boundaries in Baranes: the difference between finishing an action and establishing its effect, who owns that effect determination, and what strong evidence can — and cannot — prove.
